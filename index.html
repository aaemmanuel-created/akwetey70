Our theme

The Glory of the Lord’s House

Our theme for the celebration

“The glory of this latter house shall be greater than of the former, saith the LORD of hosts: and in this place will I give peace.”Haggai 2:9
“With long life will I satisfy him, and shew him my salvation.”Psalm 91:16

As we give thanks for seventy years, we lift our eyes to the house God is building: a house of many rooms, raised through a people, an institution and a life.

Through Dr Akwetey

A life given to scholarship, governance and service, which we honour and celebrate with gratitude.

Through the Ga people

A heritage whose symbols speak of vigilance, wisdom, gratitude and unity, carried from generation to generation.

Through IDEG

The Institute for Democratic Governance, a house of service committed to strengthening Ghana’s democratic life.

Heritage

Ga Samai: Symbols of the Ga People

Like the Adinkra of the Akan, the Ga Samai carry proverbs and values. Eight of them, and the values they carry, are gathered here.

Asrafoi

Army · military

Vigilance and unity of purpose are crucial for success in life.

Ashinɔ

Beads

Togetherness and unity.

Adashi

Appreciation

Appreciation is very important: a symbol of gratitude.

The Ga State

Antelope upon the elephant

Intelligence and wisdom are vital for effective leadership and rule.

Hiŋmɛi Sɛɛ

The eyebrow

However long the beard grows, it cannot match the eyebrow in seniority: respect for elders and the value of growth.

Akpokpolonto

The tortoise

Patience and peace are indispensable to human life.

Akromadio

Nations depend on one another

No one holds all knowledge or stands alone; we rely on one another.

Atoobi

A tiny bird

Everyone deserves respect, whatever their appearance or circumstances.

Meanings as recorded by the Endangered Alphabets Project and The Ghana Report.

The crest

Unveiling the Family Crest

The new crest of the Akwetey family, drawn from the Ga Asrafoi.

The Akwetey Family Crest

Vigilance · Unity · Purpose

  • The Asrafoi. A heart and leaves gathered and tied by one bow: a family held together by a single purpose, watchful and united.
  • The A at its heart. The initial of the Akwetey family, placed at the centre of all it holds dear.
  • The double ring. One circle enclosing the whole family, unbroken from generation to generation.
